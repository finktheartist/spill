# Attribution

[Documentation index](README.md)

## Catalog data

`src/data/rebrickable.json` contains a compact snapshot of part names, numbers, colors, and dimensions derived from [Rebrickable](https://rebrickable.com/downloads/). `scripts/catalog-sync.mjs` records how the snapshot is regenerated. Rebrickable is the source of catalog records and hosted part images.

## Geometry sources

Several mold definitions are adaptations based on LDraw part sources. Source references, authors, and license information remain in the geometry modules. The [bulk mold attribution manifest](bulk-mold-attribution.json) records source files, authors, hashes, and licenses for the bulk adaptations.

Preserve these source notices and the applicable third-party licenses when redistributing or modifying their derived data. The manifest identifies the relevant Creative Commons attribution licenses for each listed source.
